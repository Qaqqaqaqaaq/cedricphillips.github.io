Math Projects

Hierarchical dependence models for binary data

In this ongoing research under the supervision of Dr. Nešlehová and Dr. Valiquette, I am studying a hierarchical model for Bernoulli data & learning how to generate samples from the model. The goal is to develop methods to infer the tree structure of the model from Kendall and Spearman rank correlation matrix data, and explore applications of the model to credit risk. I also hope to investigate links between the model and a stochastic representation of the Farlie-Gumbel-Morgenstern distribution in terms of multivariate Bernoulli r.v.s. When the report is complete, it will appear here.

Scoring optimization of the Lizard Cult in the asymmetric game Root

This project aims to model the game state of the asymmetric board game Root from the perspective of the Lizard Cult faction, who have a unique scoring method using gardens. I do this using Markov chains, and attempt to derive optimal strategies. I also use Monte Carlo methods to simulate these policies in Python (matplotlib, NumPy). This work will hopefully appear (after a review by graduate students) this April in the 9th issue of The Delta-Epsilon, McGill's undergraduate mathematics journal.

Proof formalisation in the Lean language

I was part of a Directed Reading Program (DRP) group at McGill along with Gabriella Chen and Eloïse Freydier, guided by (now-Dr.) Jérémie Turcotte. We learned how Lean uses type theory, and taught ourselves how to prove basic facts in Lean (sometimes, the 'easier' stuff is harder than the 'hard' stuff). By the end of the program, we had formalized our own proofs of several results from Analysis 1 & 2. I would highly recommend the DRP to anyone!

Canards in slow-fast dynamical systems

As an Honours student, I had to complete a research project on nonlinear dynamics. I chose the topic of canards, a phenomenon where the tiniest variation of a parameter can cause the dynamics of a system to wildly explode. I also explored some advanced techniques in the study of canards, then summarized all my readings in a report.
