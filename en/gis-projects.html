You can view some of my projects on my SpatialNode.

Spatially aware prediction of house prices (GEOG 506)

For my GEOG 506 research project, supervised by Prof. Sieber, I developed Spatially Aware k-Nearest Neighbours (SA-kNN): a variant of kNN regression that accounts for spatial autocorrelation by down-weighting neighbours that carry redundant information, then adds a regression-kriging correction to recover the spatial structure left in the residuals. I applied it to around 12,000 residential house sales in Franklin County, Ohio, and compared it against standard spatial prediction methods.
