Translation of Adivasi Poetry

I had the unique opportunity under the supervision of Dr. Pozza at the Université de Lausanne (UNIL) to translate some works of poetry by Adivasi authors from India's Jharkand state. These works were translated from Hindi into French, and consider themes such as environmentalism, education, and political empowerment.
