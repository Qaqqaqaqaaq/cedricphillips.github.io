What does "Qaqqaqaqaaq" mean?

My Github username is a word in the Kalaallisut [kalaːɬːisʉt] (West Greenlandic) language. It is a polysynthetic language, composing words by joining several morphemes, leading to 'sentence-words' like:

qaqqaq -qa -qi -voq
mountain have emph 3.ind
'There are many mountains'
How does this happen? qaqqaq loses the final -q before suffixes like -qar-. In turn, -qar- loses its final -r- before the suffix -qi-, which when combined with -voq has a standard shortened form -qaaq.

Why is it my Github username? It seemed convenient at one time, and I come from a place with many mountains!
